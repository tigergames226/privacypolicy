Data & Account Deletion

Developer / Publisher: Tiger Games  |  Support: tigergames226@gmail.com

This page provides step-by-step instructions on how to delete your game data or submit a data deletion request for any mobile game or application published by Tiger Games on Google Play.

1. How to Delete Local & Cloud Game Data Directly

Most games developed by Tiger Games do not require you to create an external account and store your gameplay progress directly on your device or through your Google Play Games account. You can delete this data immediately at any time:

  1. Delete Local App Data (Android):
    Open your device's Settings > Apps > select the [Tiger Games App] > Storage & cache > tap Clear storage / Clear data, or simply uninstall the application.
  2. Delete Google Play Games Cloud Data:
    If the game uses Google Play Games Services for cloud saves or leaderboards, open the Play Games app on your device > tap the three dots (More) at the top > Settings > Delete Play Games account & data, then click Delete next to the specific Tiger Games title.
  3. Reset or Delete Your Advertising ID:
    To unlink analytics and advertising identifiers, go to your device's Settings > Google > All services > Ads, and select Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

3. Data Deletion & Retention Policy

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety requirements, below is a summary of what data is deleted and how retention is handled when you request deletion:

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Player/Account identifiers linked to our backend (if applicable to the game)
  • Cloud-saved progress stored on Tiger Games servers
  • Customer support emails and contact records
  • Identifier-linked diagnostic or analytics records under our direct control

Data That May Be Retained

  • Aggregated / Anonymized Data: Non-identifiable gameplay statistics and crash summaries that cannot be linked back to any individual device or user.
  • Legal & Transaction Records: Google Play purchase receipts or records required for tax, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Processing Timeframe: All verified data deletion requests are processed and permanently completed within 7 to 30 days of receipt.
