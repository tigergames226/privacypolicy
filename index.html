Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 27, 2026  |  Publisher: Tiger Games

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile games, applications, and related services published by Tiger Games on the Google Play Store and other app marketplaces.

1. Overview

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices concerning the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you download, install, and play our games.

We design our games to be fun and engaging without collecting unnecessary personal information from our players.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the specific game you are playing, the following types of information may be processed:

  • Non-Personal & Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, locale/country settings, network status, and unique device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID).
  • Gameplay & Performance Data: Game progress, high scores, levels completed, session duration, in-game achievements, crash logs, and diagnostic reports to help us fix bugs and improve performance.
  • Information You Voluntarily Provide: If you contact us for customer support, we receive your email address and any details you provide in your message.

Note: We do not collect or store sensitive personal information such as your real name, physical address, phone number, or government IDs.

4. In-App Purchases & Payments

If any of our games offer virtual items or in-app purchases, all financial transactions are processed directly by the Google Play Store billing system. We never receive, process, or store your credit card or financial details.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting the privacy of young children is especially important to us. Our general audience games do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions).

If a game is specifically targeted toward families or children, any integrated advertising networks are configured in COPPA-compliant mode (showing only contextual, non-personalized advertisements). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to remove it.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain user databases with personal identities. Game save data is typically stored locally on your device or linked to your Google Play Games account (which you can manage directly in your Google Account settings).

To delete local data, you can simply clear the app's cache/data via your device's Settings > Apps > [Game Name] > Storage or uninstall the game. If you wish to request deletion of any support email history or identifier logs, contact us at the email below.

7. Security

We prioritize the security of any data processed. While no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is 100% secure, we utilize industry-standard practices and rely on trusted cloud infrastructure (such as AWS and Google Cloud) to safeguard user information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our games or relevant laws. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Any updates are effective immediately upon being posted on this page.
